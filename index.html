Research Focus

Sustainable energy storage materials, understood in real time

The group develops key materials for next-generation rechargeable lithium-ion and sodium-ion batteries. We combine materials synthesis, electrochemical testing, advanced structural characterization, and especially in-situ and operando methods to understand reaction mechanisms that govern battery performance.

Current directions include high-energy lithium-ion cathode and anode materials, high-performance sodium-ion cathodes, solid-state batteries, and electrochemical in-situ characterization.

Selected Directions

From fundamental mechanisms to practical materials

Lithium-ion batteries

Layered Ni-rich cathodes, phase behavior, fast charging, interfacial stability, and single-particle reaction heterogeneity.

Sodium-ion batteries

Layered oxide cathodes, anion-redox chemistry, low-state-of-charge phase transitions, and long-life sodium storage.

In-situ characterization

Operando and real-time probes that connect structural evolution, ion transport, and electrochemical behavior.
