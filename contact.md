## Open Positions

The group welcomes applications from postdoctoral researchers, PhD-track students, master's students, and undergraduate students with backgrounds in chemistry, physics, materials science, or related areas.

Candidates interested in electrochemical energy storage are encouraged to email a CV, research interests, and a short background introduction to Assist. Prof. Chao Xu.
