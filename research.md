## Research Focus

The group develops key materials for next-generation rechargeable lithium-ion and sodium-ion batteries, and uses advanced characterization tools to reveal the reaction mechanisms that control electrochemical performance. Our goal is to connect fundamental understanding with practical battery materials for a sustainable and carbon-neutral society.

Major research directions include:

- High-energy-density cathode and anode materials for lithium-ion batteries
- High-performance cathode materials for sodium-ion batteries
- Solid-state battery materials and electrolytes
- Electrochemical in-situ and operando characterization methods

## Lithium-Ion Batteries

We study Ni-rich layered cathodes and related materials with a focus on structural evolution, phase behavior, surface reconstruction, fatigue, fast charging, and reaction heterogeneity. Representative work includes:

- Bulk fatigue induced by surface reconstruction in layered Ni-rich cathodes for Li-ion batteries. *Nature Materials* 20, 84-92 (2021). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41563-020-0767-8)
- Operando visualization of kinetically induced lithium heterogeneities in single-particle layered Ni-rich cathodes. *Joule* 6, 2535-2546 (2022). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joule.2022.09.008)
- Phase Behavior during Electrochemical Cycling of Ni-Rich Cathode Materials for Li-Ion Batteries. *Advanced Energy Materials* 11, 2003404 (2021). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/aenm.202003404)

## Sodium-Ion Batteries

We investigate layered sodium cathodes, including anion-redox materials and phase transitions at low state of charge, with an emphasis on long-life sodium-ion batteries. Representative work includes:

- Enabling the synthesis of O3-type sodium anion-redox cathodes via atmosphere modulation. *Nature Communications* 16, 2343 (2025). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-57665-1)
- Navigating low state of charge phase transitions in layered cathodes for long-life sodium-ion batteries. *Energy & Environmental Science* 18, 6032-6042 (2025). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5EE00422E)

## Solid-State Batteries

The group is expanding research on solid-state battery materials, including lithium-ion-conductive electrolytes and interfaces that enable safer and higher-energy storage systems.

## In-Situ Characterization

In-situ and operando techniques are central to our work. We use real-time characterization to observe structural changes, phase transformations, ion heterogeneity, and degradation pathways as batteries operate.
